What this bot does

In plain language

The bot reads every message posted in the server and makes a judgment call in about two seconds — the same call a thoughtful moderator would make, but instantly and around the clock.

It quietly removes messages that don't belong in the community before anyone has to respond to them. That last part matters most: in the recent incident, the problem wasn't just the inappropriate messages — it was the argument that broke out when members felt they had to say something. If the messages disappear quietly, nobody has to speak up, and the conflict never starts.

Your words, built in: "automatically remove it… just prevent any conflict if someone did speak up about it." — that's the design goal of the whole system.

What it catches

  • Sexual or romantic targeting — flirting at you or members, body/clothing comments, "I want you", kiss/lip-bite emojis, creepy patterns that build up over several messages
  • Profanity — all cussing, including disguised spellings like "fk", "sh1t", "b!tch", and in other languages too
  • Hate speech — slurs and degrading language of any kind
  • Self-harm talk — flagged immediately so someone can check on the person
  • Brewing arguments — veiled threats like "I'll back up my words with actions", goading, and hostility between members, caught while it's still small

What it leaves alone (just as important)

  • "You're beautiful" / "you're so talented" / "great stream today" — all safe. Kind words are the heart of the community and the bot scored 100% at never touching them.
  • Gaming trash talk — "let's fight", "get wrecked" — safe, it knows the difference
  • Faith talk — "the sermon was about heaven and hell", "Jesus saves us from damnation" — safe, it understands religious language isn't cursing
  • Jokes and slang — "I'm dead 💀 that was hilarious" — safe

What happens when something is caught

The message is deleted instantly, and the person gets a gentle, private note — no public embarrassment: "Hey! 👋 Just a heads up — your message was removed. No big deal, just keep things friendly and mindful of others. You're good! 🙂"

Repeat offenders get short automatic timeouts that escalate (10 min → 1 hr → 24 hr), and moderators see everything in a private log channel plus a live web dashboard.

How it thinks

✅ VerdictSAFE — nothing happens
CAUTION — logged for mods, message stays
REMOVE — deleted + gentle DM

Context is the whole trick

The same words can be innocent or inappropriate depending on what came before. The bot reads each sender's recent messages so it judges the pattern, not just one line:

MessageOn its ownWith creepy context
"I'm waiting for you"SAFE — waiting for a streamREMOVED — right after "just showered" + 🫦
"missing you at stream"SAFEREMOVED — as "miss you so much 💋"
"can't wait for tomorrow"SAFEFLAGGED — after "I saved your pictures"
"come at me bro"SAFE — gaming banterREMOVED — mid-argument as intimidation

Three verdicts, three levels of response

VerdictWhat it meansWhat happens
SAFENormal community chatter, compliments, banterNothing — message stays
CAUTIONUncomfortable undertone, but not clear-cutMessage stays; mods quietly notified. Three cautions targeting the same person escalates automatically
REMOVEClearly crosses the lineDeleted in seconds + gentle private DM
Built-in safety valves: moderators and staff are never moderated; the whole system can start in "shadow mode" — where it only logs what it would do without deleting anything — so you can watch it on real chat and approve its judgment before turning enforcement on.

The real test: replaying July 23–24

We ran the actual incident through the bot, message by message. Every call was correct.

What actually happened: one member posted a series of escalating comments over an evening. Nobody removed them, so other members eventually spoke up — and the next day it blew up into an argument with profanity and veiled threats. Here's what the bot would have done at each step:

REMOVED in ~2 secMember · 7:25 PM
"Ima Go Shower and then ill be ready n waiting.. Is the Beautiful Lexi going live tonight…"
REMOVEDMember · 8:29 PM
"…mildly innappropriate but… you got some dresses… I bet they look nice on you… i still get flash backs of the Blue Jeans…"
FLAGGED — pattern noticed (3rd in a row)Member · 8:40 PM
"oh and... i love your face!"
⏸️ And here the story ends. With the 7:25 and 8:29 messages gone within seconds, the other members never see them, never feel they have to say something ("You're being weird ngl") — and the entire next-day blowup never happens. The member gets one gentle private DM instead of public embarrassment.

But to be thorough, we also tested the blowup itself — in case something similar ever unfolds anyway:

REMOVED — disguised profanityMember (next day)
"…sorry, lemme just sit in a corner… nah fk that.. that aint me"
FLAGGED — goadingMember
"furthermore.. who are you to judge? ill wait.."
REMOVED — veiled threat, caught by contextMember
"…ill gladly back up my words" → "with actions"
REMOVED — profanity + intimidationMember
"…id want it to get out of hand cuz who tf r u? u feel me?"
SAFE — bystanders never punishedOther members
"If y'all have beef deal with it somewhere else…" / "Just figured you'd be more respectful…"

Note the hardest call in the whole set: the two-word message "with actions" is harmless on its own — the bot caught it as a veiled threat because it reads each person's recent messages, not single lines.

Suggested rollout

  • Week 1 — Shadow mode: the bot watches and logs but touches nothing. You and the mods review its calls on real chat.
  • When you're comfortable — flip it on: one command. Everything shown in this report goes live.
  • Always adjustable: sensitivity (low / medium / high), which channels, timeout lengths, and the wording of the DM are all settings you control from the dashboard.
